# Changelog

Notable changes to this study kit. The format loosely follows
[Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/); dates are `YYYY-MM-DD`.

The **question-bank version** shown on the site footer tracks this file.

## [0.1.0] — 2026-05-26

First public release.

### Study content
- Five domain cheatsheets, a glossary, a scenario guide, a master trap sheet, and a study guide.
- 111 flashcards (repeat-mistake + domain coverage) with a dependency-free Anki deck builder.
- 220-question practice bank with answers and per-option explanations.

### Apps (hosted on GitHub Pages)
- Browser-native **timed mock exam**: focus picker (full mock or single-domain drill),
  one-question-at-a-time flow with no skipping ahead, 120-minute timer, scaled scoring,
  per-domain breakdown, and full answer review. Runs entirely client-side; attempts are
  stored in the browser's `localStorage`.
- Browser-native **flashcards app**: study by domain or shuffled, one card at a time, with a
  downloadable Anki deck.
- Redesigned landing page in an Anthropic-inspired theme, with no-JS fallbacks and accessible
  focus styles.

### Meta
- Exam facts (60 questions · 120 minutes · 4 of 6 scenarios · 720/1000 to pass) verified against
  Anthropic's public exam guide and Academy as of **2026-05-26**.
